Privacy policy

Updated: 13 September 2026

App and contact

This notice describes Po Robocie, an Android app for documenting upholstery cleaning. Publisher: WarebitDev. Contact for app and privacy enquiries: dev@warebit.net.

Data stored on your device

You can enter your business name and contact details, a report footer, job details, client name, address, item list, results and notes, and before and after photos. The app also records documentation reference numbers, IDs and dates. Job data and processed photos remain in the app’s local storage on your device. The publisher does not receive them automatically.

Photos and permissions

The camera starts after you choose this feature and grant the system permission. Alternatively, you can select a specific photo through the system photo picker. The app does not need access to your entire gallery. Added photos are stored as JPEG copies, limited to 1600 pixels on the longer side. Originals in your gallery remain unchanged. Photos are optional.

Scheduling and payments

You may optionally save a visit date and time, work and travel duration, job amount, total payments received (including a deposit), and a follow-up date and confirmation. This information is stored locally and included in JSON backups. The job amount, amount received and outstanding balance appear in the PDF report.

Optional phone and Google calendar

When you choose to link a visit, the app asks Android for permission to read and write calendar data. It reads available calendars and their names/accounts so you can choose one, then saves and updates a linked event with the job title, reference, client, address and reserved time including travel. It reads linked events to check for changes; when recovering an unconfirmed save, it may read events around that date. Photos, prices, payments and other job notes are not sent to the calendar. A selected Google or other synchronised calendar may send the event to its service provider and share it according to that calendar’s settings. The Po Robocie publisher does not receive this data. You can unlink a visit or revoke permission in Android settings. The one-time calendar form works without Po Robocie reading calendars and requires you to save the event yourself. Unlinking, moving to the bin, uninstalling or clearing Po Robocie data does not delete calendar events. Use the separate delete button or your calendar app to remove an event.

Messages and backup confirmation

Message templates are created on your device from job and business details. You can edit the text, then choose an app and recipient and send it yourself. The app does not send messages automatically or read contacts, SMS or your mailbox. Draft text is not saved. Calendar links and confirmation that you have kept a backup are stored separately on this device and are not exported in JSON. Backup confirmation stores a date and a digest for detecting changes; the app does not verify that the exported file still exists at its destination.

Language choice

The selected interface and report language (Polish or English) is saved in this device's settings. It is not sent to the publisher or included in JSON backups. The language selected for an individual report or quotation is part of that document and its backup. Changing the language does not translate user-entered data.

Price list, quotations and document appearance

The price list, quotation items, quantities, prices, discount, travel charge, arrangements, conversion status, business logo, aftercare instructions and document language are stored locally and included in JSON backups. Logos are resized to a maximum of 800 pixels and saved as PNG (up to approximately 500 KB). The selected language changes document labels but does not translate user text. A PDF may contain the logo and the data and arrangements entered by the user.

Maps and notifications

The navigation button sends the job address to Google Maps or a browser. Po Robocie does not read the phone's location; the selected maps app operates under its own permissions and policies. Notifications are optionally scheduled locally after Android permission is granted. They store the date, category and number of visits, contacts or payments, without client names, addresses or amounts. They do not use the publisher's server or register a push notification token. Preferences are saved only on this phone and can be disabled in the app or Android settings.

Saving a backup through a file provider

You can manually save JSON using the system location picker, including Google Drive if that provider is available on your phone. Po Robocie receives access to the selected file and confirms the write after reading back matching content. It does not obtain access to your entire Drive or Google account. The provider may upload the file to the cloud under its own policies; check the status in its app. This is not automatic synchronisation. Later deletion or damage to a backup outside the app is not monitored.

External services

The current version does not use in-app accounts, a synchronisation server, advertising, analytics or external AI services. Core features work offline. If you download the app through Google Play, Google handles the download and any purchase under its own terms; the app does not read your payment card details.

Exporting and sharing

You initiate PDF or JSON backup exports and choose where to save them or which app to share them with. A file may contain client information, notes and photos. Further processing by your chosen app is governed by its own terms. A full backup also includes business details and the bin. Previously exported files are outside Po Robocie’s control.

Retention and deletion

Documentation remains locally until you delete it or clear app data. The bin does not empty automatically: it keeps photos and notes for restoration or permanent deletion. Permanent deletion removes the job from the database and photo files no longer in use. Temporary export files may remain in the cache until it is cleared. Uninstalling removes local app data but does not remove gallery originals or exports saved outside the app.

Backups and control of your data

You can edit and delete documentation and make your own backups. The publisher cannot access local jobs and cannot recover or delete them remotely. Keep a backup outside the app before changing phones, uninstalling or clearing app data. Use a phone lock and restrict access to exports containing client information.

Contacting the publisher

If you write to dev@warebit.net, you provide the publisher with your sender address and message content to handle your enquiry. You do not need to send job backups or client data: describe the problem and use demonstration data. Contact the same address to request access to, correction or deletion of data provided in correspondence. Correspondence is used to handle the enquiry and is retained for the time needed to resolve it, and longer only where justified by legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. You may ask the publisher about the retention period for your enquiry.

Changes to this notice

This document is available in the app offline. Its content should be updated before releasing features that change data processing. The version date appears at the start of the document.
