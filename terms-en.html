Purpose and publisher

Po Robocie, published under the name WarebitDev, is an Android app for documenting upholstery cleaning services. Contact: dev@warebit.net. These terms describe the current version’s operation and conditions of use.

Access and charges

The pilot version distributed by the publisher outside Google Play is free. It does not start a subscription or automatic payment after a trial period. For a version distributed through Google Play, the price and download terms are presented in the store before any purchase. The current app does not offer in-app purchases.

Use and documentation

You may use the app to document your services. Add only material for which you have the necessary rights and permissions. Describe results accurately and process client information and data relating to people shown in photos lawfully. Do not use the app to impersonate others or falsify a service record.

Meaning of the report

The PDF report presents information entered by the user. Before sharing it, check the photos, descriptions, recipient details and completion status. The app does not assess cleaning quality, confirm client acceptance of the service or include a client signature. The report does not guarantee the outcome of a complaint or dispute.

Payments, messages and reminders

The job amount and total payments are entered by the service provider. Payment status follows these amounts; the app does not check a bank account or process payments. A PDF report is not an invoice. Check the message and recipient before sending it through your chosen app. Any SMS or data charges depend on your mobile plan. The contact list and backup reminder are available when you open the app. Optional system notifications for the schedule, follow-up contacts and payment deadlines require enabling them and granting Android permission. The system or battery saving may delay them. The schedule covers the next 200 future notifications and refreshes after changes and when the app opens. Past notification times are not replayed. After force-stopping the app, you need to open it again.

Linked calendar visits

Calendar linking is optional. Changes saved in Po Robocie are sent to the linked event while the app is running or when it is reopened. Calendar changes are not automatically imported into the job. A conflict requires the user to choose an action. Google sync, calendar access and event reminders depend on phone settings and service availability. Plan detects overlapping open Po Robocie jobs with a start time and work duration; it does not compare them with private events outside the app. Importing a backup on a new device does not transfer calendar links. Check existing events before linking again to avoid duplicates.

Quotations and documents

A quotation contains prices and terms entered by the service provider and is neither an invoice nor a payment receipt. Check quantities, prices, discount and travel before sharing. Accept a quotation after agreeing it with the client; the app does not independently verify their consent. Saved quotations keep their prices regardless of later price list changes. Version 2 JSON backups require app version 0.7 or newer; importing version 1 backups remains available.

Requirements and limitations

The app is intended for Android 7 or later; feature availability also depends on the device and its settings. A job can contain up to 10 items, each with one before and one after photo. A JSON backup can be up to 50 MB; larger archives can be exported as individual job backups. The camera and other sharing apps operate according to Android settings.

Saving and backups

Data and photos are stored locally without synchronisation to the publisher’s server. Regularly make backups outside the app and check that they can be read. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local documentation. Unsaved-form warnings help during navigation, but the system may close the process without showing a warning. Do not ignore save errors.

The pilot version is intended to collect feedback and may contain errors. A feature plan is not a promise to deliver features by a particular date.
